# Example prompts

Questions a producer actually has, in their own words. A connected assistant
picks the tools; none of these need you to know a tool name.

## Getting through the week

- What compliance gaps do I have right now?
- Which of my scenes still need something before I can close them?
- Is production PROD-2026-014 ready to finalize?
- What paperwork expires in the next 30 days?
- Which scenes are waiting on a performer to accept an invite?
- How many performers on scene 4 have a verified ID on file?

## Certificates and proof

- A platform sent me this certificate code. Is it still valid?
- Which of my productions have a current certificate, and which were
  superseded?
- Give me the archive hashes for my last finalized production.

## Back catalog and imports

- Show me every scene in my imported back catalog, including the clean ones.
- Which imported records are missing something a new record would have?

## Your account

- What plan am I on and how much of my allowance is left?
- Are there any removal requests linked to my records?
- What paperwork does a performer need, and what does a crew member need?

## Solo creators

- List my content logs from last month.
- What is the evidence state behind LOG-9F3A21B7?

## What it will not do

These are outside the connector on purpose. Ask and it will point you at the
site:

- Activate a scene, or close one out.
- Sign or attest to anything.
- Buy, upgrade, or change a plan.
- Show you a performer's name, ID, or documents.
